About the Art

A tradition as old
as celebration itself

Mehandi, also known as Henna, is one of India's most beloved art forms — a centuries-old tradition of adorning hands and feet with intricate patterns made from the natural henna plant.

Every design tells a story. From the delicate florals of a bride's hands to the bold geometric patterns of a festival, our artists bring your vision to life with a steady hand and a heart full of tradition.
